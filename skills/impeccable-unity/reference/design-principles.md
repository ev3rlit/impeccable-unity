# Design authority and scope

The brief wins. Honor pinned aesthetics, eras, materials, fonts and palettes even when a common-pattern warning would otherwise apply. The project’s explicit DESIGN.md and user decisions remain authority. A skill is not permission to replace their taste.

Refinement preserves the incumbent identity, behavior, factual copy and everything outside scope. Redesign preserves product truth, functions, native affordances and constraints, but replaces the old visual world instead of polishing a discarded one. Missing documentation alone does not mean missing identity: inspect code, tokens, assets and current screens first. Changes to factual claims require evidence and authorization; do not add invented customers, statistics, testimonials, costs or licensing.

Modes describe the surface, not the entire product: **Persuade** earns a decision with real evidence; **Operate** helps complete a task with precision and familiar affordances; **Read** helps understanding and navigation; **Experience** lets the artifact lead. A game's shop may require a different emphasis than its HUD. Resolve the mode from the task and preserve binding art direction; never force a website aesthetic onto a game.

Keep harmful loaded emblems out of decoration: the subject's world does not license supremacist, militarist or hate motifs such as Nazi insignia, the Rising Sun flag's rays or the Confederate battle flag. Factual content documenting such symbols is distinct from ornament. Choose neutral material from the subject's world.

Policy and capability are independent. A supported effect may still be forbidden for this use. Keep each original policy's conditions and exceptions; do not reinterpret all category defaults as unconditional bans or remove a ban because Unity can render it. A different renderer is not a loophole. Implementation constraints may change the technique, never secretly the approved design intent.

PRODUCT.md owns confirmed product truth. DESIGN.md owns durable visual system decisions. A screen brief owns its task and direction. Avoid copying internal direction contracts, research notes or technical implementation explanations into player-facing labels, UXML comments, localization strings or shipped assets. Use existing development-only locations for needed design artifacts; do not invent audit/history files.
